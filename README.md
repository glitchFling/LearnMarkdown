## Lesson 4: Alert Cards

this one may trick you a little bit, but if you slow down, take some time, you'll get through it.<br>
It's not guarranted tho.

Alert Cards (officially called Alerts or Callouts by GitHub) are native blockquote extensions that let you emphasize critical notes, tips, warnings, or urgent information inside your markdown documentation files.

For example;

> [!NOTE]
> Go outside and touch grass.

> [!TIP]
> Take a walk outside instead of staring at a screen for 10,000 hours.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Knowledge.

> [!WARNING]
> If i don't learn or study, i might fail! (common sense)

> [!CAUTION]
> "Negative consequences of an action or severe security risks." - gemini ai
